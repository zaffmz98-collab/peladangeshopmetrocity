About Peladang Eshop

Empowering Local Farmers, Delivering Fresh Quality to MetroCity

Peladang Eshop MetroCity is an initiative dedicated to connecting local agricultural producers directly with urban communities. Established with a vision to provide healthy, fresh, and affordable local products, we serve as MetroCity’s primary hub for daily fresh produce and Sarawakian specialties.

Every purchase you make supports local farming families, sustainable agricultural practices, and the local economy.
